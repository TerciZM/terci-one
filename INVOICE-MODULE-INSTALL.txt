TERCI ONE — INVOICES MODULE

1. Open Cloudflare Dashboard > D1 > your Terci database > Console.
2. Run migration-invoices.sql once.
3. Upload or replace all matching files from this ZIP in the GitHub repository.
4. Keep functions/api/invoices.js inside the functions/api folder.
5. Wait for the Cloudflare Pages deployment to finish.
6. Open https://terci-one.pages.dev/invoices.html

INCLUDED FUNCTIONS
- Invoice dashboard and saved-invoice register
- All, Draft, Sent, Unpaid, Overdue, Paid and Void views
- Search and pagination
- Receivables and due-date summary
- Customer search from D1
- Item search from D1
- Editable quantity, unit and rate
- Automatic due date and totals
- Draft and Sent saving
- Edit, void, restore and delete
- Invoice preview and browser PDF download
- Server-side daily sequential invoice numbers

NOTE
The "Save and mark sent" action records the invoice as Sent. Email delivery will be connected when the email workflow is added.
